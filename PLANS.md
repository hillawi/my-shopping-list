# Plans

Keep this file up to date every time a plan-gated feature is added, changed, or removed —
it's the source used to fill in the Play Store listing (free vs. in-app purchase details).

## Free

- One household per account
- Up to 2 members per household
- Real-time shared shopping list
- Purchase history for the last 30 days

## Paid

Everything in Free, plus:

- Up to 10 members per household
- Unlimited purchase history
